About Me

I’m a GIS analyst with a background in geology and a focus on cartography, spatial analysis, and web GIS applications. My experience in mineral exploration, mining, and public land management informs how I collect, interpret, and communicate geographic information.

After taking time away from the workforce to raise my family, I earned a Certificate of Proficiency in Geospatial Analysis from Weber State University in 2026. Today, I create maps and applications that support planning and help people understand the places around them. Outside work, I enjoy hiking, foraging, rockhounding, and spending time with my family.

Skills

  • Mapping and analysis: ArcGIS Pro, cartographic design, spatial analysis, and geospatial data preparation
  • Web and mobile GIS: ArcGIS Online, Experience Builder, Instant Apps, Field Maps, and Survey123
  • Geology and fieldwork: Geological mapping, field data collection, geological databases, and environmental assessments
  • Currently learning: Python for GIS automation; HTML, CSS, and JavaScript

Work Experience

As a Research Analyst Fellow at the Utah Governor's Office of Planning and Budget, I support statewide planning through GIS. My work includes building and maintaining the BUILD: Utah Statewide Vision, bringing planning information from agencies and community partners together in one place.

In my past life as a geologist, I worked for the Solid Minerals Division of the Bureau of Land Management at the Carlsbad Field Office. Before that, I worked as a mineral exploration geologist for different companies on precious metal, base metal, & industrial mineral projects in Alaska, New Mexico, Arizona, and Ontario.
