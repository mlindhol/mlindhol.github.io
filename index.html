About Me

I am a GIS Analyst who enjoys making attractive and useful maps, learning new things, foraging in the Utah wilderness, camping, hiking, rockhounding, and spending time with my family.

After years as a mineral exploration and mine geologist, I left the workforce 11 years to raise a family. In 2026, I obtained a Certificate of Proficiency in Geospatial Analysis at Weber State University and embarked on a second career as a GIS professional. I absolutely love making maps and solving geospatial problems! While I currently create web apps using ArcGIS Online software, I'm also learning html, css, and Javascript so that in the future I will be able to build custom apps to host my web maps.

Skills

I am proficient in a wide range of Esri software products. I work with ArcGIS Pro every day, performing data engineering tasks, geospatial analyses, and designing map layouts to print. I also use ArcGIS Online daily to create and maintain web maps, Experience Builder apps, Instant Apps, Field Maps apps, and Survey123 surveys. I have basic skills in Envi remote sensing software, and I am on a journey to improve my grasp of python to improve and automate GIS tasks and to learn html, CSS, and Javascript for web development. In January 2027, I look forward to indulging my love of Excel formulas and conditional formatting in a Data Analytics and AI course through the Tech Moms program. Strengthening my skills in non-geospatial data analysis, building dashboards, and learning effective use of AI will help me produce more well-rounded web apps that bring spatial and non-spatial data together in effective ways.

While I have not worked as a geologist since returning to the workforce, the skills and experience I acquired working as a geologist for mineral exploration and mining companies as well as the BLM give me the advantage of understanding how geospatial data is acquired in the field, and how and why it is used in both industry and government. As a geologist, I have a background in:

  • Geological mapping and field data collection
  • Geological database development and data management
  • Core and rock-chip logging
  • Environmental assessments, mine inspections, and core-hole permitting
  • Geochemical studies and scientific sample collection

Work Experience

I currently have a challenging and rewarding internship as a Research Analyst Fellow at the Utah Governor's Office of Planning and Budget. This is an incredible opportunity for a rookie GIS Analyst; I get to do GIS work that has statewide impact and my position allows me to meet and learn from top GIS professionals and planners from across Utah, including at the Utah Geospatial Resource Center. Every person I have worked with does exceptional and impactful work and has a wealth of expert knowledge to share. Among other duties, I built and maintain the website and interactive map for BUILD: Utah Statewide Vision.

In my past life as a geologist, I worked for the Solid Minerals Division of the Bureau of Land Management at the Carlsbad Field Office. Before that, I worked as a mineral exploration geologist for different companies on precious metal, base metal, & industrial mineral projects in Alaska, New Mexico, Arizona, and Ontario.
