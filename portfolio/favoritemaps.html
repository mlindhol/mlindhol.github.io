I am geologist and GIS analyst. I enjoy learning new things, foraging in the Utah wilderness, camping, hiking, rockhounding, and spending time with family.

I grew up in Fergus Falls, Minnesota. After high school, I spent a year in Vaxjo, Sweden as an exchange student before obtaining a Bachelor's degree in Geology from Bryn Mawr College. I completed graduate-level coursework in geochemistry and ore deposit geology at New Mexico Tech. After working as a geologist for years, I left the workforce for 10 years to raise a family. In 2026, I obtained a Certificate of Proficiency in Geospatial Analysis at Weber State University and embarked on a second career as a GIS Analyst. I love making maps and solving geospatial problems!
